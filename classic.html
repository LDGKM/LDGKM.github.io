      About

      Mulhouse, France. Looking for a work-study position (alternance) in data or R&D for 2027–2028, or an internship from May 2027 that could lead to it.

      I studied information security in Benin for three years, then moved to Mulhouse to finish a bachelor's in computer science. This summer I worked on ConfoPE, a compliance platform for childcare facilities, at PetitAvis: a production database of more than fifty tables, a dashboard comparing facilities, and a large schema restructuring without losing data.

      I'm now in the first year of a master's (Computing & Mobility track). The projects in the notebook are what I'm proudest of from that year. What they have in common is that they start from data or rules and let you look at the result.

      I'm moving towards data analysis because the part I enjoy most is measuring things carefully and explaining what the numbers mean.
